Privacy Policy

Foreign Link Radio mobile apps · Effective September 28, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect a random device identifier, playback and screen-activity events, app diagnostics and crash data, optional shoutout display names and messages, and the approximate country associated with a submitted shoutout. The app does not require an account and does not collect contacts, precise location, financial information, health information, photos, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

We use this information to stream Foreign Link Radio, operate and moderate Shoutouts, measure reliability, diagnose crashes, and improve the service. We do not sell personal information or track listeners across other companies' apps and websites.

Sharing and security

Chosen display names and shoutout messages may be shared publicly in the app or on air when published or selected by the station. Technical data is shared only with service providers needed to operate and improve the service. Data is transmitted over HTTPS where supported; no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your choices and deletion

You can report or block content in the app. Use Settings → Delete my data to permanently erase your device record and submitted shoutouts. You can also contact Foreign Link Radio support to request deletion.

Children's privacy

The service is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if a child has submitted personal information.
