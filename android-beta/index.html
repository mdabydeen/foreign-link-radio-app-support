Foreign Link Radio app support

Join the Android beta

Use the same Google account for all three steps—the one signed in to Google Play on your Android phone.

  1. Join the FLR Beta Testers Group. Already a member? Go to step 2.
  2. Open the Google Play signup page and tap Become a tester. Check that it confirms you are a tester. Joining the group alone does not complete this step.
  3. Install Foreign Link Radio on your Android phone.

What to test

Try live playback, background listening, lock-screen controls, and the DJ lineup. Stay enrolled for at least 14 consecutive days and use the app naturally during the test.

Send feedback to vijrampersaud@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version, what you tried, and what happened. Google Play needs at least 12 testers continuously enrolled for 14 days before we can apply for a public release.

Having trouble joining?

Check that the Google Group, signup page, and phone Play Store use the same Google account. If you just joined the group, allow time for membership to update and reopen the signup page.
